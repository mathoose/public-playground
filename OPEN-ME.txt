Bubble frame is in the bubble-frame folder.

In Finder:
1. Open bubble-frame
2. To print the default 4×6: drag the three .stl files into your slicer
     bubble-frame-4x6in-frame.stl
     bubble-frame-4x6in-back.stl
     bubble-frame-4x6in-stand.stl
3. To customize sizes: double-click "Start Bubble Frame.command"
   (first time: right-click → Open)

If you do not see a bubble-frame folder here, this copy of public-playground is old.
In GitHub Desktop: Fetch origin, then Pull origin. Then look again.
