Plate

The back plate lies on the bed. Width and height are the plate. Thickness is how tall the plate is before the arms.

Hex cutouts

A honeycomb, not a square grid. Every neighboring pair is the same distance apart, so the wall is one thickness and the points do not pinch. The source through-hole was 12.4 mm flat to flat.

Outer ring

Clearance from the plate edge to the honeycomb. The source border was 21 mm on the left and right and 25 mm on the top and bottom. This ring stays at least 22 mm, and the same on the left and right, and on the top and bottom.

Arms

Four arms rise from the corners. Thickness is the same from the plate to the tip — the source arms tapered from about 20 mm down to 15 mm.

Download

Print the plate on the bed, arms straight up, 0.2 mm layers, no supports. In use the plate stands vertical and the arms stick out to hold cups.
