# Kitchen shelf cups

Parametric remix of the uploaded shelf: a back plate with hex cutouts and four corner arms.

The source through-holes were 12.4 mm flat to flat, and the border was 21 mm on the left and right and 25 mm on the top and bottom. The arms tapered. This designer starts with 24 mm hexes on a honeycomb — one wall thickness, no pinched corners — inside a ring that stays at least 22 mm. The arms keep one thickness.

Print the plate on the bed so the arms rise straight up. No supports.
