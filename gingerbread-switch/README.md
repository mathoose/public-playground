# Gingerbread switch

A US single-gang light switch cover. The plate is the normal rectangle (69.85 × 114.3 mm) so it sits on the wall. The gingerbread house is thicker than that plate and runs past its edges: roof eaves to the sides, chimney above the top. The back is flat, including the overhang, so the whole piece still lies on the wall.

The toggle comes out an arched door. A rocker option uses the Decora opening and the wider screw spacing (96.8 mm). Toggle screws stay 60.3 mm apart. Screw heads are recessed to 4 mm of plastic so a standard plate screw still reaches.

Three colors, not fused: gingerbread, icing, and candy. Separate STLs, plus one joint 3MF (a single assembly, so the icing and candy stay on the house).
