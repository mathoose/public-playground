# Gingerbread switch

A US light switch cover on a standard device-mount plate. Single toggle or rocker, or a **double toggle** plate (115.9 × 114.3 mm) with two ~14 × 32 mm openings. The gingerbread house widens for two gangs but keeps the same depth and side overhang as the single plate — sized to match the uploaded ghost toggle silhouette.

The plate is the normal rectangle so it sits on the wall. The house is thicker than that plate and runs past its edges: roof eaves to the sides, chimney above the top. The back is flat, including the overhang, so the whole piece still lies on the wall.

Toggle screws stay 60.3 mm apart (four holes on the double plate, gang centers 46.0 mm apart). Rocker uses Decora spacing (96.8 mm). Screw heads are recessed to 4 mm of plastic so a standard plate screw still reaches.

Three colors, not fused: gingerbread, icing, and candy. Separate STLs, plus one joint 3MF (a single assembly, so the icing and candy stay on the house).

`reference-front.jpg` is the drawing the relief follows. `reference-toggle-upload.stl` is the user’s decorative ghost toggle mesh (measured bbox **147.1 × 189.3 × 5.0 mm**; center **35.6, 61.4 mm**).
