A standard US single-gang plate, with a gingerbread house standing off it. The roof and chimney run past the rectangle, the back stays flat on the wall, and the screws stay on the real switch spacing. The toggle comes out the door.

Gingerbread Icing Candy 3MF

Print flat, house up, 0.2 mm layers, no supports. The back of the plate and the overhanging roof sit on the bed, which is the wall. Import the three STLs, or the joint 3MF — gingerbread, icing, and candy stay separate parts in one assembly. Screws are recessed to 4 mm so a standard plate screw still reaches. Toggle screws are 60.3 mm apart; rocker screws are 96.8 mm apart.
