Download

    The flat piece and the legs are separate. The flat piece prints on its face. The legs STL is already lying on a flat face, four legs in a row, so they print flat. The legs stay hexagonal when the openings are square. Twist lock: drop the hooks into the lead-ins, turn clockwise until they stop, and leave the clear gap under the catch. The Socket view is one socket with the leg in it, so you can see that gap as you change the fit. The other end of each leg is a plain hex seat. Press fit: hole depth sets how far the socket goes in. With the chamfer on, the mouth is wider and the hole tightens toward the floor. With it off, the hole is straight. Hole offset is how much larger or smaller that hole is than the leg. The socket test is one corner and one socket. It asks for the settings, engraves those numbers on the piece, and keeps the export in the list below so it can be loaded again. Print that first. The joint 3MF is the shelf with the legs stood in the sockets, for checking the stack. Slice the STLs to print. PETG holds a load better than PLA. 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 20% infill.
