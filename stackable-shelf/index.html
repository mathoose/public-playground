The flat piece and the legs are separate. The flat piece prints on its face. The legs STL is already lying on a flat face, four legs in a row, so they print flat. The hole-offset test is one corner and one socket, at the offset you set. Print that first and try a leg in the hole. The joint 3MF is the shelf with the legs stood in the sockets, for checking the stack. Slice the STLs to print. Press each leg into a top socket, then set the next shelf’s bottom sockets on the leg tops. PETG holds a load better than PLA. 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 20% infill.
