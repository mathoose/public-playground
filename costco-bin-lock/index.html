The caliper spanned the long opening: 32.2 mm, jaw just past the 32 mm tick. Width is the short side, about 11.4 mm from the photo. Type either number if you measure again.

Square end

The pad you twist, on top of the lid. This sets the side of that square. Measure turns on face picking: click one face, then another. The distance sits in a bubble, and the dimension line is drawn outside the part so the lock does not cover it.

Hook

Engagement is how far the tab reaches under the rim on each side. The shelf is flat, so a full bin pushing out seats the lock instead of sliding it back through the slot. The old clip was too shallow and fell out.
