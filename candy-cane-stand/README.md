# Candy cane stand

Friction-fit base for the 77 cm Giant Candy Cane. The flared foot and wall stay; the socket is bored out so the shaft can seat without glue.

Measured from the public plate meshes (millimeters):

| | mm | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shaft outer diameter, 77 cm profile | **105.0** | Bounding width of the tube. On MakerWorld 876637 profile “77 cm lang”, the cane lies in the XY plane, so the diameter is the Z span. Ten of eleven segments span 104.997–105.011 mm, with vertices at ±52.50 mm. The 110 cm profile is the same cane at 110/77 and spans 150.00 mm. |
| Original stand hole | **94.9** (fitted circle 94.86; oval 93.8 × 95.9) | Horizontal mesh cross-sections of MakerWorld 3432714 from 15 mm to 80 mm height. Circle fit radius 47.43 mm, RMS 0.36 mm. The oval matches the foot’s ~2% stretch. |
| This bore | **105.4** | Shaft plus 0.4 mm diametral clearance (inside the 0.3–0.6 mm friction band). |

Sources: [Giant Candy Cane](https://makerworld.com/models/876637) (77 cm) and [Stand for 770MM candy stick](https://makerworld.com/models/3432714). The candy cane itself is unchanged.

Print the foot on the bed, bore up. No supports. If a particular cane printed fat, raise the clearance slider and download a new STL.
