Fit

For the 77 cm Giant Candy Cane (MakerWorld 876637, profile “77 cm lang”). The straight shaft measures 105.0 mm outer diameter: the tube’s bounding width, vertices at ±52.50 mm. The original stand (MakerWorld 3432714, “Stand for 770MM candy stick”) has a 94.9 mm hole — a fitted circle of 94.86 mm, slightly oval at 93.8 × 95.9 mm — so the cane cannot enter. This bore is 0.4 mm larger than the shaft. The flared foot, 4.6 mm wall, and 85 mm height stay.

Download
Default STL

Print the foot on the bed, bore up, 0.2 mm layers, no supports. The mouth has a short lead-in; the straight bore is the friction fit (105.4 mm at the default 0.4 mm clearance). If your cane printed fat, raise the clearance before exporting.
