Bag roll

Measure your roll with calipers. Clearance is the gap per side between roll and bore.

Slot & windows

The slot runs from the top rim down into the pull window. Because it cuts through the rim, the threaded top is split and flexes instead of cracking.

Keyring tab

Radial fin with a 45° underside so it prints upright without support. Angle is measured around the body from the pull window.

Thread & cap

Raise thread clearance if the cap binds; lower it if it rattles. 0.3 mm suits most PETG/PLA printers.

Print PETG (or PLA), 0.2 mm layers, 3–4 walls, no supports. Body: flat base on the bed. Cap: exported upside down — closed top on the bed, threads up. Screw the cap on before loading the roll once to check the fit.
