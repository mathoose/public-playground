2-phone wall holder

Drag to orbit · pinch/scroll to zoom
Base

Remix of Jonesman's “Wall-Mounted Phone Holder Integrated Cable pocket” (MakerWorld #3024216), deepened to hold two phones. Personal use only, don't share the files.

Pocket

Depth is measured inside at the floor, front lip to the leaning back wall. Walls stay 2 mm, and the slot, cable clip and screw holes don't move.

Phones (preview)

Ghost phones in the viewer, used for the fit check. iPhone 15/16 Pro Max is 8.25 mm thick and 77.6 mm wide bare, and about 11–12 mm and 81–82 mm in a typical case.

Download

Print it standing on its base as the original does: PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, no supports. The extra depth is straight floor and side wall, so it adds no overhangs.
