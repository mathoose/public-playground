Lantern file

Use your copy of the With Hole pumpkin lantern. The bottom opening is resized to the whole LED disc, and a round hole is cut through the ring so the cord can leave and be plugged in. Nothing from the file is uploaded.

LED disc

Measure across the whole disc, rim included. 2.3 in is a common flat plug-in puck. Extra room is added so the disc drops into the opening.

Cord

A circle through the bottom ring. 8 mm clears a typical low-voltage lead. Raise it if the plug itself has to pass through. Turn the direction until the hole exits a rib.

Download

Print it stem-up, the same way as the original lantern, 0.2 mm layers. The opening is the bottom, so the disc sits on the table inside the ring. Paint the stem in the slicer if you want two colors. Personal use of your own file.
