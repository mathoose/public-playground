Fit

Clearance is added on every side of the head. Raise it if the head will not drop in; lower it if it rocks.

Razor head

Henson AL13 / Ti22 defaults (same head). Caliper your head and adjust — the cap sits face-down in the pocket.

Cradle

Knurled puck you hold (or set on the counter) while you twist the handle.

Handle grip

Optional fluted sleeve that stays on the handle. With the head off, slide it on from the threaded end and push toward the tail; the bore follows the handle taper so it wedges tight near the tail.

How to use
  1. Drop the razor head-first, cap down into the cradle so the handle points up. The cap’s narrow top face goes to the bottom of the pocket.
  2. Hold the knurled cradle (or set it on the counter) and turn the handle counter-clockwise (lefty-loosey, looking down at the handle tail). The optional grip sleeve gives wet hands more purchase.
  3. Lift the handle out — the baseplate comes with it or lifts off. The cap stays in the pocket blade-up; the blade edges sit in the relief gap, away from plastic and fingers.
  4. Take the old blade off by its short ends only (use the finger notches). Push the cap up through the bottom hole if it sticks.
  5. Lay the new blade on the cap tabs, put the baseplate back (bushing up), thread the handle clockwise until finger-tight.
