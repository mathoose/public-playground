Kit spec

BambooSegD LED Lamp Kit-001, the same hardware family as Bambu Lab LED Lamp 001. Designed by Lin and Gleason.

This unit
BambooSegD model M4001. DC 5V, 4W, RGB, USB, 3-pack. Read from the box label. The label does not print a puck height.
Puck size
About Ø59 mm. Official pages disagree on height. Bambu’s US store lists the MH001 puck as Ø59 × 8 mm, DC 5V, 3W, warm white. 3DJake lists that same MH001 kit as 59 × 18 mm.
How a shade fits
The shade twist-locks onto a printed base. Kit design rules: 0.2 mm gap between the base and the shade wall, and 0.1 mm interference on the locating lug.
Base and ring
LED Lamp 001 on MakerWorld — the Lin and Gleason base files and the design guide.
