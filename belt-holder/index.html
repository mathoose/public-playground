Holder

Two pins plug into the closet upright. The default is 5 mm pins on 32 mm centers, a common closet pitch — change hole spacing to match your holes.

Print with the pins on the bed and the opening up. One piece, no supports. On the closet, turn the pins into the holes so the opening faces you. The belt clip is an open C. The square bin adds side walls.
