Dollar sign

One solid dollar sign: a vertical bar and two S loops. The bar stays narrow because the card is not inside it. Width grows the loops.

Card slot

ISO ID-1 card, 85.60 × 53.98 × 0.76 mm, rotated around the broom so the card is perpendicular to the S. The long side stays vertical and the card sticks out to the side. Clearance is extra gap on each side and on the thickness. Drop the card in one shell, snap the other on, then slide the case down the dovetail.

Broom socket

Right-hand 3/4″-5 ACME female thread in the dollar-sign piece — the usual US broom and mop stick. The snap case does not have the socket. Turn the sign clockwise onto the handle. Engagement is how far that thread runs up the socket.

Case front Case back Dollar sign 3MF

Print the dollar sign with the socket mouth on the bed so the thread and the dovetail slot are vertical. Print the case halves flat; put the dovetail of the back half on the bed. Snap the case around the card, then slide it down the bar until it stops. A plain wooden dowel with no thread will not hold — this socket is threaded, not a slip sleeve. Cut a 3/4″-5 ACME on the stick, or use a broom or mop handle that already has that thread.
