Preview only. The pale card is the ISO card behind the two strokes. Two STLs stay separate. The 3MF is one assembly of those shells, already closed around the card.

Dollar sign

A large S with two vertical strokes through it, and a gap between the strokes. Width grows the S. The strokes stay narrow so the curves stay the main shape.

Card slot

ISO ID-1 card, 85.60 × 53.98 × 0.76 mm. Clearance is extra gap on each side and on the thickness. The pocket is behind the two strokes, so the front still shows the gap. Drop the card in the back shell, then snap the front on.

Broom socket

Right-hand 3/4″-5 ACME female thread in the back shell — the usual US broom and mop stick. Turn the staff clockwise onto the handle. Engagement is how far that thread runs up the socket.

Front Back 3MF

Print the back with the socket mouth on the bed so the thread is vertical. Print the front flat, outside face down. Press the four posts through until they click. The card sits behind the two strokes; the gap between them stays open. A plain wooden dowel with no thread will not hold — this socket is threaded, not a slip sleeve. Cut a 3/4″-5 ACME on the stick, or use a broom or mop handle that already has that thread.
