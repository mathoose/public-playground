Preview only. The pale card is the ISO card in the pocket. Two STLs stay separate. The 3MF is one assembly of those shells, already closed around the card.

Dollar sign

Vertical S with a bar. The bar is wide enough that the card stays inside it, and the bar sticks out past the top and bottom of the S. Width grows the hooks.

Card slot

ISO ID-1 card, 85.60 × 53.98 × 0.76 mm. Clearance is extra plastic gap on each side of the length and width, and the same extra on the thickness. Drop the card in the back shell, then snap the front on.

Broom socket

Right-hand 3/4″-5 ACME female thread in the back shell — the usual US broom and mop stick. Turn the staff clockwise onto the handle. Engagement is how far that thread runs up the socket.

Front Back 3MF

Print the back with the socket mouth on the bed so the thread is vertical. Print the front flat, outside face down. Press the four posts through until they click. The card is trapped in the bar; the S counters stay open. A plain wooden dowel with no thread will not hold — this socket is threaded, not a slip sleeve. Cut a 3/4″-5 ACME on the stick, or use a broom or mop handle that already has that thread.
