# Dollar staff

Broom-head replacement. The 3/4″-5 ACME socket is at the bottom and screws onto the handle where a plastic head came off. The head is one solid S with a vertical stroke through it. The S is four times as thick along the card. The snap case is only as thick as the stroke, the stroke is deepened so the card box stays flush, and the bottom corners of that box are sharp squares on a straight edge where they meet the stroke. The card top is even with the top of the stroke.

Open the designer from the [3D printing library](../3d-printing/). Sliders set the dollar width, the S stroke, the center bar, card clearance, and how long the broom thread runs.

- `dollar-staff-v11-case-front.stl` — snap-case lid
- `dollar-staff-v11-case-back.stl` — snap-case back, with the dovetail
- `dollar-staff-v11-sign.stl` — dollar sign, neck, and broom socket
- `dollar-staff-v11.3mf` — all three, assembled

The card is ISO ID-1 (85.60 × 53.98 × 0.76 mm) plus the clearance slider, 0.4 mm per side by default. Snap the shells together, then turn the socket clockwise onto the stick. A plain unthreaded dowel will not grip this socket.
