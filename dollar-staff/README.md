# Dollar staff

Broom-head replacement. The 3/4″-5 ACME socket is at the bottom and screws onto the handle where a plastic head came off. The head is one solid S with a vertical stroke through it, raised on a neck. The credit card is perpendicular to the S, the stroke crosses the middle of the card, and the top of the card is even with the top of the stroke. Two shells snap around the card and slide down a dovetail in the bar.

Open the designer from the [3D printing library](../3d-printing/). Sliders set the dollar width, the S stroke, the center bar, card clearance, and how long the broom thread runs.

- `dollar-staff-v6-case-front.stl` — snap-case lid
- `dollar-staff-v6-case-back.stl` — snap-case back, with the dovetail
- `dollar-staff-v6-sign.stl` — dollar sign, neck, and broom socket
- `dollar-staff-v6.3mf` — all three, assembled

The card is ISO ID-1 (85.60 × 53.98 × 0.76 mm) plus the clearance slider, 0.4 mm per side by default. Snap the shells together, then turn the socket clockwise onto the stick. A plain unthreaded dowel will not grip this socket.
