# Dollar staff

Broom-head replacement. The 3/4″-5 ACME socket is at the bottom and screws onto the handle where a plastic head came off. The solid dollar sign and the credit card sit up on a neck, clear of the stick. The card is perpendicular to the S, and the stroke crosses the middle of the card. Two shells snap around the card and slide down a dovetail in the bar.

Open the designer from the [3D printing library](../3d-printing/). Sliders set the dollar width, the S stroke, the center bar, card clearance, and how long the broom thread runs.

- `dollar-staff-v5-case-front.stl` — snap-case lid
- `dollar-staff-v5-case-back.stl` — snap-case back, with the dovetail
- `dollar-staff-v5-sign.stl` — dollar sign, neck, and broom socket
- `dollar-staff-v5.3mf` — all three, assembled

The card is ISO ID-1 (85.60 × 53.98 × 0.76 mm) plus the clearance slider, 0.4 mm per side by default. Snap the shells together, then turn the socket clockwise onto the stick. A plain unthreaded dowel will not grip this socket.
