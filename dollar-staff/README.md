# Dollar staff

Solid dollar sign that screws onto a broom, plus a two-piece snap case for a credit card. The card is turned so its long side is horizontal. A dovetail on the back of the case slides down a slot in the sign. The 3/4″-5 ACME socket is on the sign, not the case.

Open the designer from the [3D printing library](../3d-printing/). Sliders set the dollar width, the S stroke, the center bar, card clearance, and how long the broom thread runs.

- `dollar-staff-v3-case-front.stl` — snap-case lid
- `dollar-staff-v3-case-back.stl` — snap-case back, with the dovetail
- `dollar-staff-v3-sign.stl` — dollar sign and broom socket
- `dollar-staff-v3.3mf` — all three, assembled

The card is ISO ID-1 (85.60 × 53.98 × 0.76 mm) plus the clearance slider, 0.4 mm per side by default. Snap the shells together, then turn the socket clockwise onto the stick. A plain unthreaded dowel will not grip this socket.
