# Dollar staff

Broom-head replacement. The 3/4″-5 ACME socket is at the bottom and screws onto the handle where a plastic head came off. The head is one solid S with a vertical stroke through it. The snap case is only as thick as that stroke, and the stroke is deepened perpendicular to the S so the card box blends into the sign. The card top is even with the top of the stroke.

Open the designer from the [3D printing library](../3d-printing/). Sliders set the dollar width, the S stroke, the center bar, card clearance, and how long the broom thread runs.

- `dollar-staff-v7-case-front.stl` — snap-case lid
- `dollar-staff-v7-case-back.stl` — snap-case back, with the dovetail
- `dollar-staff-v7-sign.stl` — dollar sign, neck, and broom socket
- `dollar-staff-v7.3mf` — all three, assembled

The card is ISO ID-1 (85.60 × 53.98 × 0.76 mm) plus the clearance slider, 0.4 mm per side by default. Snap the shells together, then turn the socket clockwise onto the stick. A plain unthreaded dowel will not grip this socket.
