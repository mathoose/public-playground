# Dollar staff

Two-piece staff top: a vertical dollar sign with a credit card hidden in the bar, and a 3/4″-5 ACME socket that screws onto a US broom or mop handle.

Open the designer from the [3D printing library](../3d-printing/). Sliders set the dollar width, the S stroke, card clearance, the plastic around the card, and how long the broom thread runs.

- `dollar-staff-v1-front.stl` — front shell
- `dollar-staff-v1-back.stl` — back shell, with the broom socket
- `dollar-staff-v1.3mf` — both shells in one assembly

The card is ISO ID-1 (85.60 × 53.98 × 0.76 mm) plus the clearance slider, 0.4 mm per side by default. Snap the shells together, then turn the socket clockwise onto the stick. A plain unthreaded dowel will not grip this socket.
