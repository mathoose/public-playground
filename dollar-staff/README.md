# Dollar staff

Two-piece staff top: a large S with two vertical strokes and a gap between them, a credit card in a pocket behind those strokes, and a 3/4″-5 ACME socket that screws onto a US broom or mop handle.

Open the designer from the [3D printing library](../3d-printing/). Sliders set the dollar width, the S stroke, the gap between the two lines, card clearance, and how long the broom thread runs.

- `dollar-staff-v2-front.stl` — front shell
- `dollar-staff-v2-back.stl` — back shell, with the broom socket
- `dollar-staff-v2.3mf` — both shells in one assembly

The card is ISO ID-1 (85.60 × 53.98 × 0.76 mm) plus the clearance slider, 0.4 mm per side by default. Snap the shells together, then turn the socket clockwise onto the stick. A plain unthreaded dowel will not grip this socket.
