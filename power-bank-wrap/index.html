Preset

Slide-on band for Mattheus’s mint SYJ-F37F (143 × 68 × 16 mm). Cord wraps around the sleeve’s own perimeter — never over the USB end. Two elastic clips on the back hold the cord ends.

Inner hole

Width and length of the sleeve. Entry is looser; the far end shrinks by the taper on each side.

Fit

Clearance at the open (entry) end. Taper is how much smaller the far end is, per side.

Wrap around band

Two C-channels go around the 68×16 cross-section (rotated 90° from v3). Wind the cord around the sleeve itself — it cannot reach the USB end.

Cord clips

Two elastic snap clips on the back, in the valley between the wrap rings — they do not sit on the cord path. Mouth is ~58% of the cord so PETG flexes and holds tight.

Download
Print v5 default

Print PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, standing on the tight end (hole pointing up). No supports. Slide the 10+ end in first so USB / LEDs stay out. Wind the cord around the band, snap both ends into the bottom clips.
