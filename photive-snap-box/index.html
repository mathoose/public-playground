Photive snap box

Open tray + snap lid for the Photive 6-port USB brick. Cable-only holes in front; figure-8 cord slides into the rear U-slot, then the lid captures it.

Download base STL Download lid STL

Luban / PETG

  • Base: floor on the bed, open top up. No supports.
  • Lid: pretty face on the bed, snap skirt up. No supports.
  • PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 20–25% infill.
