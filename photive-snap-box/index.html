Fit

The lid is a cap: its skirt slides over the outside of the tray walls. Clearance is the gap per side between skirt and wall — higher = looser lid.

Inside

Cavity around the Photive 100×70×26 mm brick. USB extra is the plug pocket at the cable-hole end.

USB spacers

A comb drops in against the front wall, over the cables. Each tooth fills the gap behind one plug so it can’t back out. Measure each plug from the brick face to the back of its body (calipers) — 0 = empty port. Looking at the USB holes from outside the box, port 1 is the rightmost; the spacer has a corner notch on its port-1 end.
